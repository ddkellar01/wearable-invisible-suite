# Wearable Textile Assembly Guide

## Overview
This guide covers the integration of flexible micro-OLED and e-paper weave panels onto the custom wool-blend tactical suit, ensuring concealed internal wiring conduits[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

## Materials Required
- Custom wool-blend suit with pre-stitched internal channels[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)
- Flexible micro-OLED panel modules
- Micro-coaxial wiring harnesses
- Low-profile heat-shrink tubing

## Assembly Steps
1. **Conduit Routing**: Feed micro-coaxial data lines through the reinforced internal sleeves along the arms, legs, and torso.
2. **Panel Integration**: Secure micro-OLED tiles into the exterior mesh pockets. Ensure strain relief on all header pins.
3. **Thermal Testing**: Power on the diagnostic dashboard to verify no thermal throttling occurs along the wiring channels.
