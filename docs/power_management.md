# Power Management & Battery Distribution

## Specifications
- **Power Source**: Dual 12V Li-Po slim battery packs[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
- **Expected Runtime**: Approximately 3 hours of active cloak time under standard operating loads[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

## Safety & Distribution Protocol
- Balance charging must be performed prior to deployment.
- The onboard Jetson Orin Nano monitors current draw via telemetry pins. If draw exceeds safety thresholds, auxiliary display zones dim automatically.
