# Safety & Operational Guidelines

1. **Thermal Limits**: Ensure ambient airflow around the processor housing sink. Maximum operating temperature is 75°C.
2. **Electrical Isolation**: Never operate the suit in heavy precipitation without confirming seal integrity on all micro-camera mounts and power connectors.
3. **Emergency Power Cutoff**: Locate the quick-release magnetic battery disconnect on the lower back belt line for instant power termination.
